Design for defensibilityPrefer the simplest chart that answers the question, label units, and avoid decoration that changes the reader's inference.
Comparing categories?Use bar, grouped bar, lollipop, slope, ranged dot, or pyramid charts depending on labels and comparison structure.
Tracking time?Use line, area, sparkline, range area, bump, calendar heatmap, Gantt, or candlestick charts depending on grain and uncertainty.
Explaining composition or flow?Use treemap, sunburst, donut, stacked bar, waterfall, Sankey, funnel, or network forms only when their structure is the point.
